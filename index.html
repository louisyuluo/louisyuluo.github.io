01About

Strategy, differentiation, and the audiences who judge it.

I am a doctoral researcher in Strategy and Management at Imperial College London. My dissertation develops a theory of contrarian strategy: strategies that challenge, rather than follow, the prevailing consensus in a market.

A firm can be distant from its peers without contradicting them. I separate these two components of differentiation, model when consensus-challenging strategies attract capital, and test the predictions across venture funding, peer review and patent data. A third study looks inside the head of the strategist, using think-aloud protocols to see where contrarian choices come from.

02Research

Working papers

  1. When Contrarian Strategy Attracts Capital: An Audience-Contingent Theory of Investable Value

    A parsimonious formal model that defines contrarianism, distinguishes it from distance-based differentiation, and derives when audiences reward strategies that challenge the consensus.

  2. Disagreeing with the Market, Disagreeing about the Venture

    An empirical study of audience disagreement: how investors evaluate ventures whose strategies contradict market consensus, and why such ventures split opinion.

    Working paperEmpiricalVenture funding

  3. Thinking Against the Grain: A Think-Aloud Study of Strategy Formulation

    Using think-aloud protocols from 80 sessions to trace how individuals arrive at consensus-following or consensus-challenging strategies.
